English, on device. First use downloads about 65 MB total: the speech model and audio runtime. Audio is not uploaded. Word confidence is not supplied by this model, so review every word and timestamp.

If the model cannot run, add words and time offsets manually. This does not run speech recognition.

Choose a local audio or video pad and opt in to begin.

Combine the current phrases into a new, editable story without deleting the originals. Reorder its words, preview or export WAV.

Map a phrase into separate local WAV pads and a one-bar pattern at the current tempo. Existing samples and patterns stay untouched; the layout needs enough empty pads with unused patterns.

Melody writing

Build a note sequence with pitch, step, and length. The default tuning reference is A4 = 440 Hz; a custom reference changes local tone previews. MIDI export follows the standard A4 = 440 Hz tuning. Assign phrase words to notes as pitched vocal sample cues, or add a semitone-shifted harmony line and edit it note by note. Word cues support MIDI pitches 48–72; harmony cues outside that range are skipped.

Live note tuner

Rap writing

Draft 16 bars against the session tempo. Syllable counts and end-word rhymes are rough spelling-based hints; read and perform the lines to check the flow.

Open FLOW on a bar to place syllable starts on the 16-step grid. Audition the click rhythm locally, then export the lyrics with your flow map.

04 / OVERDUB STUDIORECORD · LAYER · MIX

Vocal overdubs

Use the beat grid below as a backing track. Recordings are stored as separate, editable local layers. When you are ready, move to Finish & Export to capture the full mix.

Start the sequencer to record a vocal layer.

Generated harmony layers reference the original local take; no raw recording is overwritten. A pitch processor is required to keep timing aligned. Edit harmony pitch, gain, mute and solo independently.
